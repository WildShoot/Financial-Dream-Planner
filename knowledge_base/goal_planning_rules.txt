Financial goals should be planned according to income, savings and goal timelines.
Short-term goals usually require less time and should use safer investment options.
Long-term goals provide more time for investment growth.
If the required monthly investment is higher than the available saving capacity, the user may increase the goal timeline.
A financial shortfall can be reduced by increasing savings, increasing income or extending the goal timeline.
Users should prioritize important financial goals based on urgency and affordability.
